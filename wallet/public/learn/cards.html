Fund card and convert back

Load the explicit sample reserve, select an asset and review a demo token-to-USD quote. Confirmation debits the synthetic reserve and credits the card's demo balance. Converting unused balance back credits the synthetic reserve. These operations do not sign, bridge, sell, withdraw or transfer real crypto.

Are the displayed card details usable?

No. Any full number is documented public sandbox test data. Expiry and CVV are synthetic demo values. They are not credentials for a live card and cannot authorize real purchases.

Does a frozen card still spend?

The demo blocks purchases and funding/conversion actions that its frozen-state rules forbid. In a real product, the issuer must enforce authorization rules server-side; local browser state is not a financial security boundary.

What would real card funding need?

A licensed or partnered issuer/program, eligible cardholder enrollment, a secure backend, provider-managed card details, transaction authorization, settlement, fraud controls, refund/reversal handling and reviewed conversion/on-ramp services. Eligibility and rules depend on the provider and jurisdiction.

Demo limitations

Demo quotes are reference values, not executable market prices or guarantees that a stablecoin is redeemable for one USD. Browser storage can be modified by its owner, so the demo ledger is an interaction model, not an auditable financial account. Reloaded, expired or already-used quotes must be rejected by the demo; actual money controls belong in a trusted provider/backend.

No usable cards are issued. Testnet assets cannot fund real payments. No token allowance is granted by the card demo.
