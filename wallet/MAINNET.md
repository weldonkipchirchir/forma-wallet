# Mainnet support

Select a clearly labelled Mainnet in the Network picker, or open Networks and filter Environment to Mainnets. There are 19 Mainnets (16 EVM, Bitcoin, Solana and read-only TRON) and 15 test networks. Mainnet spends real funds. This engineering prototype has not received an independent audit. Inclusion is not a guarantee of endpoint availability or support for every asset and transaction format.

## Bitcoin Mainnet

Native BTC uses BIP84 `m/84'/0'/0'/0/0` and a `bc1q` address, separate from Testnet3 `m/84'/1'/0'/0/0`. Only this single receiving/change address is scanned; restoring a phrase does not discover other accounts, paths or previously used change addresses. Legacy and Taproot destinations, RBF and token protocols are unavailable. Keep the original wallet available when testing restoration of an existing Bitcoin wallet.

The bounded, credential-free [Blockstream Esplora API](https://github.com/Blockstream/esplora/blob/master/API.md) is pinned to Bitcoin's genesis hash. Funding checks raw previous-transaction hashes, output values and ownership scripts. Reviews bind recipient, amount, inputs, change and fee. At most 20 confirmed inputs, manual rates of 1–100 sat/vB, 546-satoshi dust policy and a 100,000-satoshi fee cap are supported. Fee selection does not estimate confirmation time; immature coinbase outputs and other policy failures may still be rejected by the network.

Sending requires an account Web Lock, a review younger than 90 seconds, fresh genesis and unspent-input checks, exact signed-intent validation and a durable unknown record before one broadcast attempt. Unknown/pending submissions block subsequent sends. Missing status never proves failure. Mainnet and Testnet journals are separate. Local activity includes submitted transfers; complete history is linked to the explorer. Validation has not sent real BTC.

## TRON Mainnet — read-only

Mainnet receives and tracks TRX and standard TRC-20 balances. Issuer-listed USDT `TR7NHqjeKQxGTCi8q8ZY4pL8otSzgjLj6t` is included automatically with pinned metadata. Other contracts can be imported or partially discovered. Mainnet and Nile use separate caches but the same derived T-address, which does not identify the network.

The [TronGrid endpoint](https://developers.tron.network/docs/connect-to-the-tron-network) is fixed to `https://api.trongrid.io`; genesis follows [TRON TIP-474](https://github.com/tronprotocol/tips/blob/master/tip-474.md). Responses are bounded, timed out, credential-free and redirect-blocked. Public access may be rate limited or require a provider account; no API key is placed in the browser. Signing, sending, approvals, staking, swaps and real card funding are unavailable. This read-only adapter cannot spend received funds; an independently compatible wallet is needed. Full history is available through the explorer.

## Solana Mainnet

A phrase-backed wallet derives its existing Solana address at `m/44'/501'/0'/0'`. Devnet and Mainnet use the same address but separate balances, tracked mints and transaction journals. Ethereum-only private-key imports and BIP-39 passphrase imports cannot open this workspace. Back up the encrypted wallet before using it. Do not reuse the public phrases shown in tests or screenshots.

SOL and official Tether USDT appear automatically. The USDT mint is `Es9vMFrzaCERmJfrF4H2FYD4KCoNkY11McCe8BenwNYB`, from [Tether's issuer list](https://tether.to/en/supported-protocols/); the classic token program and six decimals are checked. Add other classic SPL mints under Tokens, or choose Find held tokens. Discovery is partial and tracking is capped at 25 mints. Display balances total validated classic token accounts owned by the address, including non-associated accounts. Sending remains associated-account-only. Token-2022 assets are unsupported. Unverified tokens retain generic identity and unavailable pricing. SOL/USDT USD values use Coinbase snapshots and are not guaranteed executable quotes or a guaranteed dollar peg.

Receive shares your wallet address, never a mint address. Send & receive supports native SOL and TransferChecked SPL transfers. The recipient must already have the associated account for the token; this release does not create it or pay its rent. Keep SOL for network fees. Reviews show the complete destination, mint, quantity and fee, and require explicit acknowledgement. Self-transfers are rejected. The fee safety limit is 0.001 SOL.

The adapter defaults to the [official public Mainnet RPC](https://solana.com/docs/references/clusters), `https://api.mainnet.solana.com`. A manual connection selector also offers [PublicNode](https://solana.publicnode.com/), `https://solana-rpc.publicnode.com`. Both are pinned to genesis `5eykt4UsFv8P8NJdTREpY1vzqKqZKvdpKuc147dw2N9d`. Responses are bounded to 2 MB with a 15-second timeout, no credentials/redirects or automatic retries. Public RPC is rate-limited and is not a production service guarantee; no API credentials are included in the client.

Before broadcast: recheck identity, 90-second review age, blockhash expiry, mint/accounts, balances and fee under an account Web Lock; reconstruct signed intent, verify signatures and simulate signed bytes. Persist the signature as unknown before making one sendTransaction attempt (`maxRetries: 0`). Pending/unknown entries block new sends across tabs. Refresh checks signature status. A missing status is never automatically treated as failure; inspect the recorded signature on the explorer before retrying. Expired unknown records remain blocked rather than risking a duplicate.

Activity lists locally submitted transactions and links to complete explorer history. Cards remain synthetic demos and cannot spend Mainnet SOL or USDT. Ledger, contacts/watch-only, swaps, bridges, staking and token-account creation are not implemented for Mainnet.

Validation uses public synthetic test vectors and mocked RPC only. Native SOL and USDT review/sign/simulation/journal flows were exercised without any real broadcast. Live funded transfers, provider availability in the user's browser, independent security review and physical-device testing remain unverified.

## EVM mainnets and tokens

Ethereum (1), BNB Smart Chain (56), Polygon (137), Avalanche C-Chain (43114), Base (8453), Arbitrum One (42161), OP Mainnet (10), Linea (59144), Unichain (130) and Ink (57073) use frozen, operator-reviewed HTTPS presets. Select the exact network at the sender. Your EVM receiving address is shared; chain balances, contracts, journals and send locks are separate. Adding a token never bridges it.

Additional presets: World Chain (480), Soneium (1868), Gnosis (100, XDAI gas), Sonic (146, S gas), Celo (42220, CELO gas) and Scroll (534352). Official references are linked in Networks. Celo alternative fee currencies and custom transaction types are unsupported; ordinary type-2 transactions pay CELO. Unconfigured native USD prices remain unavailable. Issuer-listed USDC is also included on World Chain, Sonic and Celo; other contracts remain manually importable, subject to standard ERC-20 support.

Circle-issued USDC is included on Ethereum, Polygon, Avalanche, Base, Arbitrum, OP, Linea, Unichain and Ink. Issuer-listed USDT is included on Ethereum and Avalanche. Addresses and decimals are pinned from [Circle](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/usdc-contract-addresses) and [Tether](https://tether.to/en/supported-protocols/). Imported or cached metadata cannot override the registry. Known logos and USD estimates depend on the chain/contract, never its symbol. USDC/USDT use indicative Coinbase snapshots, not an assumed dollar peg. BNB Chain does not receive a misleading issuer-native stablecoin preset; bridged tokens can be imported after verifying the exact contract and bridge provenance.

Other standard ERC-20 contracts can be imported or found through the existing partial recent-event discovery (250 tracked maximum). This is not Trust Wallet's complete backend asset index or a promise to find every holding. Native fees remain ETH, BNB, POL or AVAX as appropriate. Contracts, metadata, balances and simulation are rechecked before signing. Legacy empty-return ERC-20 transfers such as Ethereum USDT are accepted when the call succeeds; false or malformed results remain rejected. Simulation does not prove intended side effects for a malicious token.

BNB uses explicit type-0 gas-price transactions; other presets use type 2. Both signed formats are compared against the reviewed chain, sender, recipient, calldata, amount, nonce and fee fields. The account lock, durable unknown journal, pending-send block and bounded transport apply. Mainnet approval creation, mainnet Ledger, swaps, bridges, staking, NFTs and arbitrary RPC URLs are unavailable. Public RPC endpoints are rate limited; their inclusion does not establish availability in your browser or a production SLA.

L2 reviews show maximum execution fee and explicitly exclude additional data fees; a complete L2 fee quote is not implemented. Different transaction families require their own validated adapters. TON, XRP, Cardano, Cosmos and other unsupported chains are not enabled by adding RPC presets. This is not every Trust Wallet network. Demo cards cannot spend these assets.

Benchmark: Trust Wallet Chrome extension 26.45.1 was acquired from Google's official update endpoint for its published extension ID and inspected offline with REA. Eight selected source files were analyzed; the extension was never installed or executed. This partial static inspection is not a Trust Wallet audit, full runtime reconstruction or feature-parity claim. Complete evidence and limitations are retained outside this repository.

## Recovery, saved wallets and missing deposits

Restore defaults to Ethereum account index 0. Advanced recovery options retain other indexes and BIP-39 passphrases for existing wallets. Never change derivation paths just to make a balance appear; compare the complete original receiving address. Forma currently uses Solana m/44'/501'/0'/0'; another wallet may select a different Solana path even for the same phrase.

Create another wallet is available under Saved wallets on the access screen and Recovery & import. It generates a separate recovery phrase, preserves existing encrypted wallets and requires its own backup. Switching wallets locks the session. Mainnet/testnet are network selections, not separate recovery phrases.

EVM Deposit missing checks a public transaction hash on the selected chain, matches standard incoming ERC-20 events to the current account and offers verified-metadata contract import. Events do not prove issuer authenticity or current balance. Internal native transfers require explorer inspection. Solana native SOL needs the exact receiving address and Mainnet selection; importing a token cannot fix a native-SOL display problem. Try the alternate reviewed connection if RPC reads are unavailable. Providers are selected manually, never retried automatically after broadcasting.

## Environment and recovery interface

The global Environment control pairs Mainnet/Testnet variants of the selected chain and applies to all wallet pages. Network choices and overview shortcuts are filtered to that environment. A chain without a supported counterpart switches to Ethereum with an explicit notice; no new unsupported network is invented. Switching invalidates reviews through the normal navigation/session generation checks. Transfer confirmations continue to name the actual network even though repeated Mainnet suffixes are removed from ordinary live-asset cards.

Restore offers numbered 12/24-word masked fields, full-phrase paste into a word field, and Advanced options for existing account indexes/passphrases. New wallet creation displays numbered recovery words immediately and asks for three positions from the user's offline copy. The phrase is hidden after 60 seconds, discarded from pending UI state on locking/navigation, and is never written as plaintext to persistent storage or automatically copied to the clipboard. The encrypted vault is saved before the backup screen so closing/locking the page does not lose the newly generated wallet; it can be recovered through the password-protected Recovery page. This confirmation is a user reminder, not proof that an offline copy is secure.

Solana provider selection lives under Connection options. PublicNode is an alternate transport, not a different token registry or blockchain. Wallet and overview balance/account/status reads can fall back once to the other reviewed provider after verifying its genesis. Broadcast and simulation methods do not fail over or retry automatically. Changing the selected connection invalidates reuse of an overview snapshot; failed network scans are not reused as fresh cached results.

## Cross-network overview and backup checks

Overview scans the current unlocked account across the selected environment only. It includes native assets, tracked contracts/mints and reviewed issuer presets; it does not guarantee discovery of every token, other derivation paths or Token-2022 assets. Failed reads remain unavailable. USD totals are priced subtotals, with explicit coverage. Public snapshots are cached per wallet object and environment for 60 seconds; Refresh bypasses the cache. Two network workers bound concurrency, and navigation cancels outstanding work. RPC services receive public account addresses.

Creating a wallet from Saved wallets also displays its numbered phrase. Copy phrase and Verify clipboard copy are explicit actions. Clipboard verification compares locally and never displays unrelated clipboard contents; permission failure leaves word confirmation available. A matching clipboard does not establish a safe backup. Three randomized word-position questions and the safety acknowledgement confirm the user's backup step, rather than introducing personal security-question recovery.

Overview offers All networks or the selected network only; the priced subtotal follows the chosen network scope, while asset search and hide-zero filter only the list. Scope preference persists across navigation. All networks still means the selected environment, never a combination of live and test balances. Send/receive always uses one explicit chain. The unlock screen links directly to new-wallet creation, which generates a phrase without requiring an existing phrase. Chain icons are bundled locally and kept separate from gas-token icons; source attribution is in NETWORK-ASSETS.md.

## Direct swaps, card purchases and portfolio coverage

Swap signs locally for Ethereum USDT/USDC only, using Uniswap v3's reviewed Ethereum SwapRouter, factory, QuoterV2 and the 500 fee tier. It does not sign serialized transactions returned by an aggregator. The adapter checks chain ID, contract availability, pool availability, six-decimal token metadata, balances, allowances and pending/latest nonce agreement. Slippage choices are 0.1%, 0.5% and 1%; the router calldata binds exact input, output token, minimum received, current-account recipient and a deadline. Reviews expire after 60 seconds. Each approval reset, finite exact-amount approval and swap requires a separate explicit confirmation. No unlimited approvals are created; existing larger allowances are disclosed. Approvals can remain after a cancelled or failed swap and may need revocation with a compatible tool.

Before signing, the adapter rechecks the quote/minimum, allowance, balances, nonce, simulation and gas cap. It decodes the signed bytes against the reviewed transaction, uses the existing account/chain Web Lock and strict submission journal, persists an unknown record before a single broadcast, and blocks further sends while an outcome is pending/unknown. The maximum execution fee cap is 0.02 ETH and input is limited to 1,000,000 tokens. Portfolio snapshots are invalidated after submission attempts. Other chains and token pairs are not enabled for direct swaps. This is not an independent smart-contract or implementation audit; no production trade was performed during development tests.

USDT is not a universal fee asset. Forma does not implement a paymaster, relayer or USDT gas sponsorship. Transfers and direct Ethereum swaps require the native fee asset. Provider-specific gas-included features are distinct integrations, not a network-selector setting.

Buy USDT prepares a receiving address and opens MoonPay's or Transak's allowlisted public purchase page only after the user acknowledges the receiving network. It does not embed a checkout, collect card data, prefill a public wallet address into a third-party URL, sign purchases or promise geographic eligibility. The user chooses the same chain and completes any card/identity checks on the provider. Demo cards do not fund real purchases.

Solana USDT and issuer-listed USDC are included automatically. Overview and Solana holdings also discover up to 250 positive classic SPL mints and read owned account balances without silently persisting new tokens. Persistent custom tracking retains its 25-mint cap. Token-2022, NFTs, other derivation paths and full indexing of all EVM contracts remain outside coverage. The allocation chart groups only priced positive holdings, displays five networks plus an Other group, and hides with balances; it is not a historical performance chart.

Research references: [Uniswap Ethereum deployments](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v3/deployments/v3-ethereum-deployments), [Circle USDC addresses](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/usdc-contract-addresses), [Tether protocols](https://tether.to/en/supported-protocols/), [MetaMask gas-included transactions](https://support.metamask.com/manage-crypto/transactions/metamask-gas-station), [MoonPay USDT purchase](https://www.moonpay.com/buy/usdt).


## Cross-chain integration preparation (signing disabled)

Across networks is a **quote preview**, not a working bridge send. No bridge signer or broadcast code is connected to this screen. The server endpoint `/api/bridge` proxies fixed Relay quote, chain-registry and status URLs; a missing `RELAY_API_KEY` returns an unavailable state without requesting a provider quote. The current local policy allows native EVM source assets and native EVM/Solana/Bitcoin destination assets across 18 candidate networks. This is an allowlist, not a promise of provider route availability. ERC-20/SPL inputs, Solana/Bitcoin source signing and TRON bridging are not implemented. The existing same-chain Ethereum USDT/USDC adapter remains separate.

Preview validation checks Relay settlement v1 order hashing, the pinned solver signature, exact input, recipient/currency, minimum output (0.5% slippage policy), refund recipient, expiry, canonical deposit address, one explicit deposit transaction, and absence of calls/extra actions. Unknown versions, solvers and deposit-address routes fail closed. Public Relay runtime pins and a bytecode verifier are prepared for future work, but are **not run by the quote-only UI**, so the screen does not claim live contract verification. Protocol chain identifiers currently come from the provider registry; independent chain-ID pins, end-to-end quote compatibility, live contract verification, simulation, source signing, refund/delivery reconciliation and an independent review are still required before enabling funds movement. These are validation gaps, not evidence of a working live bridge.

Research: [Relay API keys](https://docs.relay.link/references/api/api-keys), [Relay input validation](https://docs.relay.link/references/api/api_core_concepts/input-validation), [Relay settlement schema and artifacts](https://github.com/relayprotocol/relay-settlement). No production quote, provider credential or funded transaction was used in development verification.

## Price reliability and Solana activity

USD estimates first try Coinbase Exchange, then race Coinbase retail spot and Kraken public ticker responses. Pair IDs, finite positive values and bounded response sizes are checked; stablecoins are not assumed to equal one dollar. Unknown token names are not used as price identities. All sources can still fail, be blocked in a region/browser, or report different prices. Refresh prices retries estimates without rescanning every network. Totals remain partial priced subtotals, not executable trade values.

Solana Activity reads signatures and parsed transactions, including incoming native transfers and classic SPL balance changes. It checks the wallet address plus up to five currently discoverable owned classic token accounts, fetches five signatures per address per page, deduplicates signatures, and bounds transaction reads to two workers. Up to 200 rendered records are retained per session. It is not complete indexing: closed/older/additional token accounts, Token-2022, unsupported transaction versions, pruned RPC data and provider errors may be absent or unavailable. Complex changes are labelled net balance changes; failed transactions never show successful transfer amounts. Other chains retain their existing history coverage; this release does not add a universal multichain history indexer.
